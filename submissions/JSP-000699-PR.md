## Problem and proposed change

Problem ID: **JSP-000699** / Erdős Problem 843.

The current catalog records the problem as **Solved** but lists
`Lean proof: No`.

This correction registers an already-public complete Lean proof that appears to
be missing from the catalog, preserves its upstream attribution, and marks the
entry **Pending verification** rather than asserting eligibility.

The pinned source proves the full catalog statement: for every two-coloring of
the positive square values, every sufficiently large natural number is a sum of
distinct square values of one color.

Pham**. The pinned Lean source names **Codex** and **GPT-5.6 Sol** as formal
authors. The submitter **Sprie** claims neither mathematical nor formalization
authorship; the contribution of this PR is locating the omitted source,
checking statement correspondence, pinning it, and reproducing the build/audit.
