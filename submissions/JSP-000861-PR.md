## Problem and proposed change

Problem ID: **JSP-000861** / Erdős Problem 1038.

The current catalog records this problem as `Open` with `Lean proof: No`.

The proposed correction records a public 2026 complete-solution manuscript and
the corresponding complete Lean formalization:

- manuscript author: **Shouqiao Wang**;
- the pinned integrated Lean metadata attributes the imported informal proof to
  **Shouqiao Wang and GPT-5.6 Sol**, building on Terence Tao's upper bound and
  reductions;
- the same pinned Lean metadata attributes the formal proof to **GPT**, model
  version unspecified.

This correction **does not adjudicate first-solution priority or award
eligibility**. Other public 2026 solution claims exist, so the change is
deliberately limited to recording this pinned public claim and formalization for
maintainer review.

The theorem scope matches the catalog problem exactly: admissible objects are
nonconstant monic real polynomials whose roots, counted with multiplicity, are
all real and contained in `[-1,1]`; the quantity is the Lebesgue measure of
`{x : ℝ | |f(x)| < 1}`.

    infimumLength = ENNReal.ofReal L ∧
    supremumLength = ENNReal.ofReal (2 * Real.sqrt 2)

The underlying definitions require:

- `f.Monic`;
- `f ≠ 1`;
- every root counted by `f.roots` to lie in `Set.Icc (-1 : ℝ) 1`;

special case or weaker bound.
