## Statement correspondence

The short JSP-000861 description asks:

> For a monic real-rooted polynomial, how long in total can the real intervals
> on which its absolute value is less than one be?

The corresponding Formal Conjectures / Erdős 1038 formulation ranges over
nonconstant monic real polynomials whose roots are all real and contained in
`[-1,1]`, and asks for the extrema of the Lebesgue measure of

`{x : ℝ | |f(x)| < 1}`.

The original manuscript states the same normalized problem. Its abstract says
that Tao established the supremum `2√2`, and presents a determination of the
previously unresolved infimum.

The integrated proof uses the matching admissibility conditions and defines
the same strict unit-sublevel measure.
