## Problem and proposed change

Problem ID: **JSP-000613** / Erdős Problem 745.

The current catalog records the problem as **Solved** but lists
`Lean proof: No`.

This correction registers an already-public complete Lean development for the
second-largest component in the random-graph transition regime and marks the
entry **Pending verification** rather than asserting eligibility.

The pinned source explicitly distinguishes the literal critical model
`G(n,1/n)` from the fixed noncritical models `G(n,λ/n)`.  At the critical
parameter it proves that the second-largest component has order
`n^(2/3)` in probability.  The same development also proves the logarithmic
first-order law away from the critical point.
