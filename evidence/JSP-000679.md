## Catalog problem

**JSP-000679 / Erdős Problem 823**

The catalog asks which positive real numbers can be approximated arbitrarily
closely by ratios of positive integers having equal sums of divisors. At the
reviewed awards revision the entry is `Solved` but records `Lean proof: No`.

## Located public formalization

A complete public Lean development is available in `plby/lean-proofs`.

- Repository: https://github.com/plby/lean-proofs
- Pinned commit: `8822f7ddef30fadbd92e1c6ab4ed897af356af5e`
- Source: `src/latest/ErdosProblems/Erdos823.lean`
- Main theorem: `Erdos823.erdos_823`
- Lean: `v4.33.0`
- Mathlib: `v4.33.0`

The theorem proves Pollack's full answer: every real `α ≥ 1` is the limit of
quotients `n_k / m_k` of positive integers satisfying
`σ(n_k) = σ(m_k)` term by term. This directly covers the catalog question.

The ComparatorChallenges manifest selects `Erdos823.erdos_823` and permits
only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.
