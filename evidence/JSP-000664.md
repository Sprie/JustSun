# JSP-000664 existing-proof evidence registration

## Catalog problem

**JSP-000664 / Erdős Problem 807**

The catalog asks how many complete bipartite graphs are typically needed to
partition the edges of a random graph. At the reviewed awards revision the
entry is `Solved` but records `Lean proof: No`.

## Located public formalization

A complete public Lean development is available in `plby/lean-proofs`.

- Repository: https://github.com/plby/lean-proofs
- Pinned commit: `8822f7ddef30fadbd92e1c6ab4ed897af356af5e`
- Source: `src/latest/ErdosProblems/Erdos807.lean`
- Comparator-selected theorem: `Erdos807.not_erdos_807`
- Stronger quantitative theorem: `Erdos807.alon_bohman_huang`
- Lean: `v4.33.0`
- Mathlib: `v4.33.0`

The original conjecture says that, asymptotically almost surely in the
uniform random graph `G(n,1/2)`, the bipartition number equals
`n - α(G)`. The selected theorem proves this conjecture false. The same
development formalizes the stronger Alon--Bohman--Huang improvement, giving an
absolute positive constant `c` (instantiated by `1/1000`) for which the
bipartition number is asymptotically almost surely at most
`n - (1+c) α(G)`, together with a logarithmic saving bound.

The ComparatorChallenges manifest selects `Erdos807.not_erdos_807` and
permits only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.
