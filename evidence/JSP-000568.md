# JSP-000568 full-proof evidence registration

## Catalog problem

**JSP-000568 / Erdős Problem 697**

The catalog asks whether the density of integers divisible by a divisor
`d ≡ 1 (mod m)`, with `1 < d < exp(m^α)`, exhibits a sharp phase transition
as `m → ∞`.

The mathematical result is Hall's theorem: the critical exponent is
`β = 1 / log 2`; below it the density tends to zero and above it the density
tends to one.

At the reviewed awards revision, JSP-000568 is recorded as `Solved` but
`Lean proof: No`.

The public theorem states the full corrected phase transition:

- `1 < 1 / Real.log 2`;
- for every `α < 1 / Real.log 2`, `δ(m, α) → 0`;
- for every `α > 1 / Real.log 2`, `δ(m, α) → 1`.

The source notes that the two limit directions in an older upstream
Formal Conjectures declaration were reversed; the completed theorem uses the
directions matching the prose statement and Hall's theorem.
