# JSP-000704 existing-proof evidence registration

## Catalog problem

**JSP-000704 / Erdős Problem 851**

The catalog asks whether a density-one set of integers can be represented as
a power of two plus an integer with a bounded number of distinct prime factors.
At the reviewed awards revision the entry is `Solved` but records
`Lean proof: No`.

## Located public formalization

A complete public Lean development is available in `plby/lean-proofs`.

- Repository: https://github.com/plby/lean-proofs
- Pinned commit: `8822f7ddef30fadbd92e1c6ab4ed897af356af5e`
- Source: `src/latest/ErdosProblems/Erdos851.lean`
- Main theorem: `Erdos851.erdos_851`
- Lean: `v4.33.0`
- Mathlib: `v4.33.0`

The theorem proves the source-faithful lower-density formulation: for every
`0 < ε < 1`, there exists a finite bound `r` such that integers of the form
a power of two plus a number with at most `r` distinct prime factors have
lower density at least `1 - ε`.

The ComparatorChallenges manifest selects `Erdos851.erdos_851` and permits
only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.
