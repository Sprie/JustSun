# JSP-000586 existing-proof evidence registration

## Catalog problem

**JSP-000586 / Erdős Problem 718**

The catalog asks what average-degree scale forces a subdivision of a complete
graph of prescribed order. At the reviewed awards revision the entry is
`Solved` but records `Lean proof: No`.

## Located public formalization

A complete public Lean development is available in `plby/lean-proofs`.

- Repository: https://github.com/plby/lean-proofs
- Pinned commit: `8822f7ddef30fadbd92e1c6ab4ed897af356af5e`
- Source: `src/latest/ErdosProblems/Erdos718.lean`
- Main theorem: `Erdos718.erdos_718`
- Lean: `v4.33.0`
- Mathlib: `v4.33.0`

The theorem gives a universal real constant `C > 0` such that every nonempty
finite graph with at least `C r² |V|` edges contains a subdivision of `K_r`.
The implementation takes `C = 5`, equivalently giving an average-degree
threshold of order `r²`.

The source also exposes the natural-number form
`containsCliqueSubdivision_of_edgeCard` and the cross-multiplied
average-degree form `containsCliqueSubdivision_of_averageDegree`.

The repository's ComparatorChallenges metadata selects
`Erdos718.erdos_718` as the terminal theorem for Erdős Problem 718.
